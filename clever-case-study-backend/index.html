Duplicate Lead Analysis

• 100% cross-source, all matched on phone — this is the key signal.
• Same sellers are submitting (or being captured) across multiple lead vendors/platforms simultaneously.
• Pattern points to cross-source lead sharing / resale — vendors aggregating and reselling the same consumer, OR sellers shopping their property across multiple sites.
• ListingLoop (5 groups) and SellFast Direct (4 groups) appear most often — likely overlapping or shared inventory between these aggregators.

2. What Ops Should Investigate First
• Audit ListingLoop and SellFast Direct contracts/feeds — confirm whether they resell or pull from shared data pools (highest overlap = highest cost risk).
• Verify whether duplicate leads carry different timestamps (re-shopping seller) vs. near-identical timestamps (vendor resale of same lead).
• Check if any duplicate pair has already been assigned to two different agents.

3. Business Risk
• Double agent assignment → two agents working the same seller, internal conflict.
• Commission/referral disputes → vendors billing twice for one lead; ambiguity over attribution.
• Wasted outreach → duplicate calls/emails annoy the seller and harm brand/conversion.
• Inflated CPL → paying multiple sources for one acquisition.

4. Recommended Actions
1. Real-time phone-based dedupe at intake — block/merge incoming leads against existing records before assignment (you're already matching on phone post-hoc; move it upstream).
2. Enforce source-of-record + attribution rules — first-touch wins for agent assignment and commission; flag later duplicates as "no-charge" and renegotiate billing with vendors who resell.
3. Vendor accountability review — quantify duplicate rate by source (start with ListingLoop/SellFast Direct), then renegotiate de-dupe credits or drop redundant feeds.
